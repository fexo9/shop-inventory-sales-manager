## What it does NOT do

- Does NOT sync between the EN and AR workbooks (separate copies — pick one).
- Does NOT record a sale when you issue an invoice — log it in Sales yourself.
- Does NOT do barcode scanning, POS hardware, or bank import.
- Does NOT produce tax reports or file taxes (simple % on the invoice only).
- Does NOT value stock by average cost or FIFO/LIFO (uses catalog cost).
- Does NOT guarantee profit, sales, or any business outcome.

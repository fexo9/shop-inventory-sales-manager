## How the stock math works (plain words)

- **Current stock** = opening stock + all stock-in qty − all sales qty.
  Example (Olive Oil): 40 + 70 − 78 = **32**.
- **Margin %** = (selling − cost) ÷ selling. Example (Olive Soap):
  (2.50 − 1.20) ÷ 2.50 = **52%**.
- **Status**: OUT at 0 or below; LOW below your threshold (default 10);
  OK otherwise. Honey sits at 14 — 4 above the threshold — so it is the
  sample shop's watch item: 5 more sales without a delivery flips it to LOW.
